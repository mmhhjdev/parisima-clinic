Everything You Need to Know About Post-Hair Transplant Care

Natural hair transplantation is one of the safest ways to restore lost hair. Learn the key steps of aftercare.

Natural hair transplants today use advanced techniques with a short recovery period, but following aftercare instructions plays a major role in the final result.

Key aftercare tips

  1. Sleeping position: For the first few nights, keep your head elevated and sleep on your back to avoid pressure on the transplanted grafts.
  2. Washing: Wash only as instructed by your doctor, using a gentle medical shampoo.
  3. Avoid strain: Avoid heavy exercise, heavy sweating and bending over for at least two weeks.

What to expect in the first week

Mild swelling around the forehead and eyes is common during the first 48 hours and fades within days. Avoid touching or scratching the treated area. Fine crusts may form after day three; gently remove them with the recommended shampoo rather than picking at them.

Returning to normal life and final results

Most daily activities can resume after about two weeks, while tight hats and long sun exposure should be limited for several months. New hair usually starts to grow three to four months after surgery, and the full result typically appears between nine and twelve months. Patience and follow-up visits are key to success.
