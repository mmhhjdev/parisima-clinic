Proven Methods to Treat Enlarged Pores on the Face

Enlarged pores are a common concern, and they can be noticeably improved with modern medical treatments such as laser, microneedling and mesotherapy.

Enlarged pores are usually caused by genetics, excess skin oil or loss of elasticity with age.

Clinical treatment options

  • Microneedling and mesotherapy: stimulate collagen and improve skin texture.
  • Rejuvenation laser: controlled resurfacing that helps tighten pores.
  • Fractional radiofrequency: stimulates deeper skin layers.

The Pari Sima team will recommend the most suitable treatment for your skin type after a specialist assessment.

Why skin type matters

Oily skin tends to have larger, more visible pores and is prone to blackheads, while dry or mature skin often shows texture changes caused by collagen loss. Identifying your skin type and the main cause of enlarged pores is the first step to effective treatment.

Home care that protects your results

Daily sunscreen is essential because UV exposure breaks down collagen and makes pores look larger. Use a gentle pH-balanced cleanser, follow your doctor’s advice on active ingredients, and avoid squeezing pores at home.
