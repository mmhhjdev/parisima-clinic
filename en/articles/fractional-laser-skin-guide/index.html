Fractional Laser for Skin: Uses, Benefits and Recovery

Fractional laser stimulates collagen through controlled micro-columns and helps improve acne scars, marks and surface pigmentation.

Fractional laser is one of the most modern dermatology techniques. Instead of destroying the entire skin surface, it creates many small, controlled columns of treatment among healthy tissue. As these columns heal, the body produces new collagen.

Main uses

  • Improving acne scars and sunken scars
  • Reducing the appearance of enlarged pores and rough texture
  • Lightening surface spots and pigmentation

Recovery

Skin usually stays red and sensitive for 3 to 5 days. Use repairing creams, avoid sun exposure and follow your doctor’s instructions.

The number of sessions and laser intensity depend on your skin type and goals. Dr. Nakhaei will examine your skin before treatment and recommend the right plan.

How fractional differs from superficial lasers

Superficial lasers mainly target the outer layer, while fractional lasers create microscopic columns that reach deeper layers and trigger more repair. This makes them especially suitable for deeper scars and texture improvement. The specialist chooses the laser type and intensity based on skin tone and thickness.

Aftercare tips

Avoid sun exposure for several weeks and use a high-SPF sunscreen. Fine flaking or temporary dryness may occur; do not pick at it. Contact the clinic if you notice blistering, infection or severe pain.
