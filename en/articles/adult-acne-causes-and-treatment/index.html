Adult Acne: Hidden Causes and Evidence-Based Treatment

Adult acne often has different causes than teenage acne. Identifying the cause is the key to effective treatment and preventing scars.

Acne after the age of 25, known as adult acne, is often linked to hormonal changes, stress, diet and the use of unsuitable skincare products. That is why treatment should address the cause, not just suppress the breakouts.

Common causes

  • Hormonal fluctuations, especially in women before their period
  • Excess sebum and clogged pores
  • Unsuitable products and insufficient cleansing
  • Stress and lack of sleep

Specialized treatment

Treatment may include topical creams and, when needed, oral medication. Controlled cleansing, mesotherapy and specialized protocols help reduce inflammation and prevent scarring.

Avoid squeezing pimples, as this increases the risk of infection and lasting scars. Early treatment is the best way to protect your skin.

Diet and adult acne

Some studies link high intake of refined sugars and full-fat dairy with more breakouts in certain people, although the effect varies. Reducing sugar and increasing vegetables and healthy proteins may help, but diet does not replace medical treatment.

Treatment duration and patience

Acne treatment usually needs six to eight weeks before results appear, and maintenance is often needed after clearing to prevent recurrence. Stopping medication early is a common cause of relapse, so follow your doctor’s plan closely.
