## Code issue fixed
`src/i18n/LanguageProvider.tsx` previously replaced metadata for every non-article route with homepage title and description after React hydration. This could overwrite the correct static metadata generated by `scripts/generate-seo.ts` for service index, individual services, articles archive, about, contact and FAQ pages.

The provider now computes route-aware title and description for article archives (including pagination), services index, individual service pages, about, contact, FAQ and homepage fallback. Article metadata remains article-specific. Canonical and hreflang handling remain in place.

## Important audit observations — not silently changed
1. Verify the generated sitemap after deployment; source `public/sitemap.xml` is a minimal fallback and the generator writes the full sitemap into `dist`.
2. Service-page hreflang assumes identical service slugs exist in all three locales. Confirm slug parity before changing the mapping.
3. Clinic geo coordinates appear in more than one source location; consolidate only after verifying the official map pin.
4. GA4 has no key events. The current event report includes 2 generic click events and 1 form_start. Configure and test explicit lead-intent events (telephone click, WhatsApp click, appointment form submission) and mark suitable events as key events in GA4. Opening WhatsApp is not a completed consultation.
5. Review medical content for evidence quality and clinician review. Avoid guarantees or categorical efficacy claims.
6. Search Console shows most measured clicks landing on the homepage. Prioritize relevant service landing pages, internal links from related articles, and useful local information.
